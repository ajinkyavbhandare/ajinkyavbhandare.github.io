I build machine-learning systems and the software around them. My work spans language models, retrieval, computer vision, inference, data processing, backend services, and infrastructure.

I work between models and the systems that make them useful — understanding what the model needs, and what the system around it needs to make it work reliably and efficiently.

  • Where does the information come from?
  • How does it move?
  • Where does the computation happen?
  • What makes it slow?
  • What happens when it fails?
  • What needs to change when the workload grows?

That's the engineering I enjoy.
